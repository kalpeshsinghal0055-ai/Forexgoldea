How does the EA stay free?+

Our partner broker pays us a commission when you open and fund an account through our link. This covers access to the EA at no extra cost to you.

Which platforms does it support?+

ForexGoldEA runs on MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5, attached to an XAU/USD chart. Free access is unlocked through our partner broker.

Do I need a VPS?+

A VPS is recommended to keep your terminal connected when your computer is off. The EA needs an active terminal and internet connection to operate.

How is risk controlled?+

You configure risk per trade, a daily loss limit and a maximum drawdown ceiling. Each position has a fixed stop-loss and take-profit. These controls do not eliminate trading risk.

The three account links above open their published histories on CopyConnectFX. Review the full history and drawdown. Historical and backtested results do not guarantee future returns.
